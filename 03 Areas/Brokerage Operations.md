# Brokerage Operations

#area #brokerage

## Purpose

Maintain reliable day-to-day brokerage operations, vendor coordination, and incident follow-through.

## Standard to maintain

- Vendor requests have an owner, reference, expected outcome, and follow-up date.
- Money-moving operations are idempotent and reconciled.
- Silent failures are detected through outcome verification.
- Operational changes leave an auditable decision trail.

## Responsibilities

- Platform and provider operations
- Funding and reconciliation oversight
- Incident coordination
- Reporting and stakeholder follow-up
