# Finance Reports

#area #finance #brokerage

## Purpose

Maintain accurate, reproducible, and reviewable financial reporting workflows.

## Standard to maintain

- Amounts use exact decimal or minor-unit representations.
- Source periods, currencies, time zones, and reconciliation rules are explicit.
- Reports identify exceptions rather than silently excluding them.
- Access and distribution follow least privilege.
