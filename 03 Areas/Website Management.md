# Website Management

#area #website

## Purpose

Maintain website correctness, availability, accessibility, content quality, and deployability.

## Standard to maintain

- Production changes are reviewed, testable, and reversible.
- Critical forms and conversion paths are monitored end to end.
- RTL presentation and keyboard/touch accessibility are verified.
- Dependencies and CMS components receive deliberate updates.

## Recurring checks

- [ ] Availability and error review
- [ ] Forms and conversion-path verification
- [ ] Dependency/CMS update review
- [ ] Accessibility and RTL spot check
